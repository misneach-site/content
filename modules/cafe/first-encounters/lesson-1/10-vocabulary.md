# Vocabulary

We've looked at quite reusable structures so far, so lets take a look at some ways that we can switch out some words for other items:

## Drinks

### Teas
- **Tae dubh** – Black tea
- **Tae glas** – Green tea
- **Tae luibhe** – Herbal tea
- **Tae le líomóid** – Tea with lemon
- **Tae le bainne** – Tea with milk

### Coffees

- **Caife dubh** – Black coffee
- **Caife bán** – White coffee
- **Meiriceánach** – Americano
- **Caifé latte** – Latte
- **Cappuccino** – Cappuccino
- **Maicéató** – Macchiato
- **Mócha** – Mocha
- **Seacláid the** – Hot chocolate

---

## Milks & Alternatives

- **Bainne** – Milk
- **Bainne leathbhearrtha** – Semi-skimmed milk
- **Bainne lánbhearrtha** – Whole milk
- **Bainne coirce** – Oat milk
- **Bainne soighe** – Soy milk
- **Bainne almóinní** – Almond milk
- **Bainne cnó cócó** – Coconut milk

---

## Add-ons

- **Uachtar** – Cream
- **Gan uachtar** – Without cream
- **Le huachtar** – With cream
- **Le leamhacháin** – With marshmallows
- **Gan leamhacháin** – Without marshmallows
- **Le siúcra** – With sugar
- **Gan siúcra** – Without sugar
- **Mil** – Honey

---

## Dietary Options

- **Véagánach** – Vegan
- **Bainne véagánach** – Vegan milk
- **Uachtar véagánach** – Vegan cream
- **Leamhacháin véagánacha** – Vegan marshmallows
- **Gan lachtós** – Lactose-free

---

## Prices

Swap these into:

> Sin ___ euro.

- **Dhá euro** – €2
- **Dhá euro caoga** – €2.50
- **Trí euro** – €3
- **Trí euro fiche** – €3.20
- **Trí euro caoga** – €3.50
- **Ceithre euro** – €4
- **Ceithre euro caoga** – €4.50
- **Cúig euro** – €5

For extra charges:

- **Deich cent breise** – 10 cent extra
- **Fiche cent breise** – 20 cent extra
- **Caoga cent breise** – 50 cent extra
- **Euro breise** – €1 extra

---

## Useful Swaps

Instead of:

> Cupán tae, le do thoil.

Try:

- **Caifé latte, le bainne coirce, le do thoil.**
- **Seacláid the, gan uachtar, le do thoil.**
- **Cappuccino, le bainne almóinní, le do thoil.**
- **Tae glas, gan siúcra, le do thoil.**

---

## Clarification Questions (Barista)

- **An bhfuil bainne coirce uait?** – Would you like oat milk?
- **Le huachtar?** – With cream?
- **Ar mhaith leat siúcra?** – Would you like sugar?
- **Rud ar bith eile?** – Anything else?
- **Beag nó mór?** – Small or large?
