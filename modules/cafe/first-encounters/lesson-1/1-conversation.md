# The Smallest, Easiest Interaction - Full Conversation
- Barista
>Dia duit! Cad is féidir liom a fháil duit? (Hello! What can I get for you?)

- Customer
> Dia is Muire duit. Cupán tae, le do thoil (Hi, a cup of tea, please)

- Barista
> Sin trí euro. Cárta nó airgead tirim? (That's three euro. Card or cash?)

- Customer
> Cárta

- Barista
> Seo duit! Tá an bainne agus siúcra ar an mbord sin (Here you are! The milk and sugar are on that table)

- Customer
> Go raibh maith agat! Slán (Thank you! Goodbye)

- Barista
> Tá fáilte romhat, slán go fóill! (You're welcome, goodbye!)
