`ceithre` (4) instead of `trí`
`haigh` (hi) or `bhuel` (well) instead of `dia duit/dia is muire duit`
`seaclaid the` (hot chocolate) instead of `cupán tae`
`bainín` (flat white) instead of `cupán tae`
